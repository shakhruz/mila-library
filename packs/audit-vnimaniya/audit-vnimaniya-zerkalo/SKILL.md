# Attention audit — a mirror, not advice

Based on Attention OS — Adrian Avendano, MIT (https://github.com/amonter/Attention-OS). The pack's LICENSE.md carries the notice.

## When
The person says they can't get to the main thing, keep getting distracted, read everything. Offer once: «Хотите, подержу перед
вами зеркало: что из того, что вы читаете и смотрите, работает на ваше дело?» «Нет» — I don't raise it again.

## Steps — one at a time
1. Their main project in one sentence (optionally: what a good result looks like).
2. 5–15 recurring inputs from the last 7 days, one per line, optionally with a feeling.
3. After the mirror: «what matches how you see it yourself?»

## The mirror — one message ≤250 words, as in SAMPLE.md
- **Helps the project** — moves it forward, gives lasting perspective or craft. **Scatters** — endless feeds, reactive news,
  «just in case» learning, short pieces that pull from one thing to another. **Useful in its time** — updates, inbox: fine in
  their window. Each item with a one-line reason tied to their project, in their words.
- Shares in % · two «matches the project» · two «leaks» · three patterns «pattern → evidence» · three questions to self.
- Missing information — an assumption in brackets. **Never** «you should», «stop», «try», steps or advice: a mirror only.
Then «does it fit?» with buttons. Saved to `~/work/library/audit-vnimaniya.md` (the owner's own; for another person on the owner's word — `audit-vnimaniya-<pd-vault label>.md`, I make the folder if it is not there); never into memory or a post.
Counting: `library.py event audit-vnimaniya started|delivered|fit:…`. ≤3 touches a day; quiet hours 22–08.
