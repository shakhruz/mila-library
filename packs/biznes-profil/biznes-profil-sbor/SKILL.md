# Business profile — one file the next packs read from

## The file
`~/work/PROFILE.md` — one page, sections in this order (empty ones stay with «—»):
`Дело` · `Клиент` · `Чем отличаемся` · `Цели на год` · then, when the person adds them: `Продукты и цены` · `Каналы` · `Цифры` ·
`Узкое место` · `Обновлено` (дата).

## How I gather it — three steps, one question at a time
1. Who the customer is — one real person, as if in front of them. 2. How they differ from the alternatives. 3. One or two goals
for the year. I write their words, not my guesses; a number they didn't name — «—». After step 3 — the whole page (SAMPLE.md),
then «does it fit?» with buttons (obratnaya-svyaz). Next step: use the profile in a proposal and on their page.
- Phones, addresses, people's names go through pd-vault labels.
- The profile is the person's file: never into a post, an export, git or another person's chat.

## Later
Any pack that needs facts about the business reads PROFILE.md first and asks only what is missing. The person says something
changed — I update the section and the date, and say in one line what changed.
